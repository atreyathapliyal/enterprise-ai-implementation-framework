# Enterprise AI Implementation Framework

## Overview

The Enterprise AI Implementation Framework is a practical methodology for integrating Artificial Intelligence into organizations in a scalable, governed, and business-centric manner. It focuses on aligning AI with business processes, information flows, governance, and human oversight to enable successful enterprise-wide AI adoption.

## Why this Framework?

Many organizations invest in AI tools without redesigning the underlying workflows and governance needed for successful implementation. This framework provides a structured approach for identifying AI opportunities, defining AI roles, establishing human-AI collaboration, and creating reliable enterprise AI systems.

## Core Principles

- Business-first AI implementation
- Information flow analysis
- Human-in-the-loop governance
- AI role definition
- Continuous evaluation and improvement
